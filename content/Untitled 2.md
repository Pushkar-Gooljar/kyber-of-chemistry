# 🗂️ Flashcard Fronts: Carboxylic Acids & Derivatives

### 1.1 Structure and Physical Properties
* What is the functional group of a carboxylic acid?
* How do the boiling points of carboxylic acids compare to alkanes and alcohols of similar relative molecular mass?
* **Explain why carboxylic acids have higher boiling points than alkanes or alcohols of similar relative molecular mass.**
* What phenomenon causes a carboxylic acid to appear to have double its expected relative molecular mass ($M_r$) in the gas phase or a non-polar solvent?
* Describe and explain the trend in solubility of carboxylic acids in water as the hydrocarbon chain lengthens.

### 1.2 Preparation of Carboxylic Acids
* What reagents and conditions are required to prepare a carboxylic acid from a primary alcohol?
* What is the colour change observed when a primary alcohol is oxidised to a carboxylic acid using acidified potassium dichromate(VI)?
* What is the colour change observed when a primary alcohol is oxidised to a carboxylic acid using acidified potassium manganate(VII)?
* What are the reagents and conditions required for the acid hydrolysis of a nitrile to form a carboxylic acid?
* **Write the general balanced equation for the acid hydrolysis of a nitrile ($R-CN$) to a carboxylic acid.**
* What are the products of the alkaline hydrolysis of a nitrile?
* **Explain why a strong dilute acid must be added after the alkaline hydrolysis of a nitrile or an ester.**

### 1.3 Reactions of Carboxylic Acids
* Write the equation to show why a carboxylic acid is classified as a weak acid.
* **Describe a chemical test, including the expected observation, that distinguishes a carboxylic acid from an alcohol or a phenol.**
* **Which of the following will react with sodium carbonate: alcohols, phenols, or carboxylic acids?**
* What are the products when a carboxylic acid reacts with a reactive metal (e.g., Na)?
* What are the products when a carboxylic acid reacts with an alkali (e.g., NaOH)?
* **State the reagents and conditions required to reduce a carboxylic acid to a primary alcohol.**
* **Explain why $NaBH_4$ cannot be used to reduce a carboxylic acid.**
* State the reagents and conditions required to form an ester from a carboxylic acid.

### 2.1 Preparation of Benzoic Acid (Aromatic)
* **State the reagents and conditions required to prepare benzoic acid from methylbenzene.**
* What observations are made when methylbenzene is oxidised to benzoic acid?
* **What is the organic product formed when propylbenzene is heated under reflux with alkaline $KMnO_4$ followed by acidification?**

### 2.2 Further Oxidation of Specific Carboxylic Acids
* **Name the two specific carboxylic acids that can undergo further oxidation.**
* **Explain structurally why methanoic acid can be oxidised further, but ethanoic acid cannot.**
* What are the final oxidation products of methanoic acid?
* **State the observations when methanoic acid is warmed with Tollens' reagent and Fehling's solution.**
* What are the final oxidation products of ethanedioic acid?
* **Write the balanced ionic equation for the redox reaction between ethanedioic acid and acidified $KMnO_4$.**
* **Explain why the titration between ethanedioic acid and $KMnO_4$ is initially very slow but speeds up as the reaction proceeds.**

### 2.3 The "Why" of Acidity (Relative Acidities)
* **State the relative order of acidity for carboxylic acids, phenols, water, and alcohols (from strongest to weakest).**
* **Explain why carboxylic acids are stronger acids than alcohols and phenols.** *(Must use exact mark scheme terminology)*
* **Explain why phenol is a weaker acid than a carboxylic acid but a stronger acid than an alcohol.**
* **Explain the effect of electron-withdrawing groups (e.g., chlorine atoms) on the acid strength of a carboxylic acid.**
* **Place the following acids in order of increasing acid strength: chloroethanoic acid, ethanoic acid, trichloroethanoic acid, dichloroethanoic acid.**
* **Explain why trichloroethanoic acid is a stronger acid than chloroethanoic acid.**
* **Explain why 2-chlorobutanoic acid is a stronger acid than 4-chlorobutanoic acid.**
* **Explain why ethanoic acid is a weaker acid than methanoic acid.**

### 2.4 Acyl Chlorides: Preparation and Reactions
* What is the general functional group structure of an acyl chloride?
* **State three different reagents that can be used to convert a carboxylic acid into an acyl chloride.**
* What are the by-products when a carboxylic acid reacts with solid $PCl_5$?
* **Explain why $SOCl_2$ is often the preferred reagent for preparing acyl chlorides in industry.**
* **What visual observation is consistently made when an acyl chloride reacts with any nucleophile (e.g., water, alcohol, ammonia)?**
* What are the products and conditions for the reaction of an acyl chloride with water?
* What are the products of the reaction between an acyl chloride and an alcohol?
* **State three advantages of using an acyl chloride rather than a carboxylic acid to make an ester.**
* **Explain why NaOH is added to phenol before reacting it with an acyl chloride to make a phenyl ester.**
* What organic product is formed when an acyl chloride reacts with concentrated ammonia?
* What organic product is formed when an acyl chloride reacts with a primary amine?
* **Explain why two moles of ammonia (or amine) are required per mole of acyl chloride in the formation of an amide.**

### 2.5 Acyl Chlorides: Mechanism & Reactivity
* **Name the mechanism by which acyl chlorides react with nucleophiles.**
* Describe the two distinct steps in the mechanism of reaction between an acyl chloride and a nucleophile.
* **Explain why acyl chlorides are much more reactive than alkyl chlorides (halogenoalkanes).**
* **State the relative order of ease of hydrolysis (reactivity with water) for acyl chlorides, alkyl chlorides, and aryl chlorides (chlorobenzene).**
* **Explain why aryl chlorides (chlorobenzene) are extremely resistant to hydrolysis / nucleophilic attack.**

# Reaction Summary
>[!tip] Reagents
>- Acidified potassium dichromate(VI), $\ce{K2Cr2O7}$ / dilute $\ce{H2SO4}$

>[!tip] Conditions
>- Heat and **distil immediately**

>[!tip] Catalyst 
>- None



# Chemical Equation
>[!summary] Equation
>$$\ce{CH2=CH-CH2OH + [O] -> CH2=CH-CHO + H2O}$$

>[!summary] By-Products
>- $\ce{H2O}$

# Observations
>[!example] Observations 
>- Orange solution turns green ($\ce{Cr2O7^2-}$ ➔ $\ce{Cr^3+}$)


# ⚠️ 9701 Exam Notes / Pitfalls
>[!warning]
>- Distil to stop at the aldehyde; reflux would give the unsaturated carboxylic acid.
>- Dichromate does not attack the C=C; avoid hot conc. $\ce{KMnO4}$ (cleaves C=C).

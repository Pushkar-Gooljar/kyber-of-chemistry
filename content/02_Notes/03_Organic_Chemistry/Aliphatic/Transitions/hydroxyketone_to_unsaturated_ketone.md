# Reaction Summary
>[!tip] Reagents
>- Concentrated $\ce{H2SO4}$ or concentrated $\ce{H3PO4}$
>- *or* pass vapour over heated $\ce{Al2O3}$

>[!tip] Conditions
>- Heat (~170 °C with acid; ~300 °C over $\ce{Al2O3}$)

>[!tip] Catalyst 
>- Conc. acid / $\ce{Al2O3}$ act as the dehydrating agent/catalyst



# Chemical Equation
>[!summary] Equation
>$$\ce{R-CH(OH)-CH2-CO-R' -> R-CH=CH-CO-R' + H2O}$$

>[!summary] By-Products
>- $\ce{H2O}$

# Observations
>[!example] Observations 
>- *No visible change* (colourless liquid/solution throughout)


# ⚠️ 9701 Exam Notes / Pitfalls
>[!warning]
>- **Dehydration**: OH is lost from one carbon and H from an **adjacent** carbon.
>- With a 3-hydroxyketone (aldol product) the C=C forms conjugated with C=O.
>- Check for **cis/trans (geometric) isomers** and for alternative positions of C=C if the OH carbon has two different neighbouring C–H carbons.
>- Tertiary OH dehydrates most easily; a tertiary alcohol cannot be oxidised, but it *can* be dehydrated.

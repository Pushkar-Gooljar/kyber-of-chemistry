# Reaction Summary
>[!tip] Reagents
>- Dilute aqueous sodium hydroxide, $\ce{NaOH(aq)}$

>[!tip] Conditions
>- Room temperature

>[!tip] Catalyst 
>- $\ce{OH-}$ acts as a base catalyst



# Chemical Equation
>[!summary] Equation
>$$\ce{2CH3CHO -> CH3CH(OH)CH2CHO}$$

>[!summary] By-Products
>- None

# Observations
>[!example] Observations 
>- *No visible change* (colourless liquid/solution throughout)


# ⚠️ 9701 Exam Notes / Pitfalls
>[!warning]
>- This is the **aldol reaction** (product: 3-hydroxybutanal, 'aldol'). Not on the core syllabus — appears in Paper 4 as an *unfamiliar reaction* where you deduce the product.
>- $\ce{OH-}$ removes an H from the carbon next to C=O; the resulting carbanion attacks the $\delta+$ carbonyl carbon of a second molecule. A new **C–C bond** forms, extending the chain.
>- On heating, the product can dehydrate to an unsaturated aldehyde (but-2-enal) — 'aldol *condensation*'.

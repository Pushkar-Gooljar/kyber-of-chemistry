# Reaction Summary
>[!tip] Reagents
>- Acidified $\ce{K2Cr2O7}$ (excess)

>[!tip] Conditions
>- Heat under reflux

>[!tip] Catalyst 
>- None



# Chemical Equation
>[!summary] Equation
>$$\ce{HOCH2-R-CO-R' + 2[O] -> HOOC-R-CO-R' + H2O}$$

>[!summary] By-Products
>- $\ce{H2O}$

# Observations
>[!example] Observations 
>- Orange solution turns green ($\ce{Cr2O7^2-}$ ➔ $\ce{Cr^3+}$)


# ⚠️ 9701 Exam Notes / Pitfalls
>[!warning]
>- The OH must be **primary** to give $\ce{-COOH}$.
>- The ketone group is **unchanged** — ketones resist oxidation by dichromate.

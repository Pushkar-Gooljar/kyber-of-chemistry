# Reaction Summary
>[!tip] Reagents
>- Steam, $\ce{H2O(g)}$
>- *or* cold concentrated $\ce{H2SO4}$, then add water

>[!tip] Conditions
>- Steam: ~300 °C, 60–70 atm

>[!tip] Catalyst 
>- Concentrated $\ce{H3PO4}$ (for steam method)



# Chemical Equation
>[!summary] Equation
>$$\ce{CH2=CH-CO-CH3 + H2O -> CH3-CH(OH)-CO-CH3}$$

>[!summary] By-Products
>- None

# Observations
>[!example] Observations 
>- *No visible change* (colourless liquid/solution throughout)


# ⚠️ 9701 Exam Notes / Pitfalls
>[!warning]
>- Treated at 9701 as ordinary electrophilic addition of water across C=C (C=O untouched), following **Markovnikov** (OH to the carbon with fewer H).
>- In reality, C=C conjugated to C=O often adds the other way round (OH on the end carbon) — an exam will usually give you the product or accept either with reasoning.
>- Check whether a **chiral centre** forms.

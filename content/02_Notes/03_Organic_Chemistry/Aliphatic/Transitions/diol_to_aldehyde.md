# Reaction Summary
>[!tip] Reagents
>- Acidified $\ce{K2Cr2O7}$ (enough to oxidise **both** OH groups)

>[!tip] Conditions
>- Heat and **distil immediately**

>[!tip] Catalyst 
>- None



# Chemical Equation
>[!summary] Equation
>$$\ce{HOCH2CH2OH + 2[O] -> OHC-CHO + 2H2O}$$

>[!summary] By-Products
>- $\ce{H2O}$

# Observations
>[!example] Observations 
>- Orange solution turns green ($\ce{Cr2O7^2-}$ ➔ $\ce{Cr^3+}$)


# ⚠️ 9701 Exam Notes / Pitfalls
>[!warning]
>- A diol with two **primary** OH groups gives a **dialdehyde** (ethane-1,2-diol ➔ ethanedial).
>- Must **distil off** the product as it forms, otherwise it oxidises further to a (di)carboxylic acid.
>- With limited oxidant only one OH reacts ➔ hydroxyaldehyde (see [[diol_to_hydroxyaldehyde]]).

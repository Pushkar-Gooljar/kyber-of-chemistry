>[!info] Core Concept 
>- Regardless of the specific reaction (nitration, halogenation, Friedel-Crafts), the core mechanism for benzene remains strictly identical.
>- The high electron density of the $\pi$-ring attracts powerful **electrophiles** ($E^+$)

## Mechanism: $S_{E}Ar$
>[!info] Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution
>It is a two-step organic reaction where an electrophile ($E^+$) replaces a hydrogen atom on an aromatic ring, restoring aromaticity in the final step.


>[!mechanism] Step 1: Attack of the Electrophile (Rate-Determining Step)
>- Two $\pi$-electrons from the delocalised ring are used to form a dative covalent bond with the electrophile ($E^+$).
>- This breaks the aromatic ring, forming an unstable, non-aromatic **intermediate carbocation**.
>
>![‎|400](2bb2d.svg)
>
>- The remaining four $\pi$-electrons are spread over the remaining five carbon atoms of the ring, in a five-centre delocalised orbital.
>
>![‎|400](f70f6.svg)

>[!mechanism] Step 2: Deprotonation (Fast Step)
>- The unstable intermediate rapidly loses a proton ($\ce{H+}$).
>- The two electrons from the $\ce{C-H}$ bond collapse back into the ring, **restoring the delocalised $\pi$-system** and the aromatic stability.
>
>![‎|400](4c741.svg)

>[!danger] Things to consider when drawing mechanism
>1. The curly arrow in **Step 1** MUST start from the *ring of delocalised electrons* and point exactly to $E^+$.
>2. The curly arrow in **Step 2** MUST start exactly *from the centre of the $\ce{C-H}$ bond* and point into the ring.

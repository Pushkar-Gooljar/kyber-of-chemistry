## 28.3 Colour of complexes

>[!info] 9701_m24_qp_42_qu_003 (3/6)
>**Question**
>- Explain why transition element complexes are coloured.
>
>**Answer**
>- The d orbital(s) split into different energy levels / d-d splitting occurs.
>- Electron(s) are promoted / excited to a higher energy d orbital.
>- Light is absorbed AND the colour seen is complementary to the frequency/wavelength absorbed.
>
>**Notes**
>- The splitting of d orbitals; the absorption of photons of visible light; the excitation of electrons and the observed colour being complementary to the colour absorbed were all described clearly.

>- Predict the relative concentrations of [CrEDTA]⁻, [FeEDTA]⁻, and [PbEDTA]²⁻ in the resulting equilibrium mixture.
>- (Given $K_{stab}$ values: Ca = 5.0 × 10¹⁰, Cr = 2.5 × 10²³, Fe = 1.3 × 10²⁵, Pb = 1.1 × 10¹⁸)
>
>**Answer**
>- Highest concentration: [FeEDTA]⁻ > [CrEDTA]⁻ > [PbEDTA]²⁻ : Lowest concentration.
>- The [FeEDTA]⁻ complex has the highest $K_{stab}$ value so it is the most stable.

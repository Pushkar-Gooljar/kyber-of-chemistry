### Question 1
Solve the inequality $|4x + 3| > 2|3x - 1|$. **[4]**

### Question 2
**(a)** Show that the equation $\log_3(3x + 7) = 2\log_3(2x - 3) - 2$ can be written as $4x^2 - 39x - 54 = 0$. **[3]**

**(b)** Hence solve the equation $\log_3(3x + 7) = 2\log_3(2x - 3) - 2$, giving your answer correct to two decimal places. **[2]**

### Question 3
A curve has equation $e^{x+4y} + x^2 + 2y^3 = 15$.
Find the equation of the tangent to the curve at the point $(4, -1)$.
Give your answer in the form $ax + by + c = 0$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers. **[6]**

### Question 4
Solve the equation $\frac{10z}{3+\mathrm{i}} + 2zz^* - 11 + 7\mathrm{i} = 0$.
Give your answers in the form $x + \mathrm{i}y$, where $x$ and $y$ are real. **[6]**

### Question 5
Find the exact value of $\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} x \tan^{-1}(2x) \, \mathrm{d}x$. **[7]**

### Question 6
**(a)** On a sketch of an Argand diagram, shade the region which represents complex numbers $z$ satisfying both the inequalities $|z + 6 - 5\mathrm{i}| \le 3$ and $|z + 3 - 2\mathrm{i}| \ge 2$. **[5]**

**(b)** Calculate the maximum value of $\arg(z + \mathrm{i})$ for points in this region. **[3]**

### Question 7
**(a)** Prove the identity $\cos 4x \equiv \cos^4 x - 6\sin^2 x \cos^2 x + \sin^4 x$. **[3]**

**(b)** Hence, find the exact value of $\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^4 x + \sin^4 x \, \mathrm{d}x$. **[5]**

### Question 8
**(a)** Express $\frac{3+x-x^2}{(2x-1)(x^2+3)}$ in partial fractions. **[5]**

**(b)** The variables $x$ and $y$ satisfy the differential equation 
$$ \frac{(2x-1)(x^2+3)}{2y} \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = (3+x-x^2), $$
and $y = 1$ when $x = 1$.
Solve the differential equation and obtain an expression for $y$ in terms of $x$. **[5]**

### Question 9
The points $A$, $B$ and $C$ have position vectors, relative to the origin $O$, given by 
$$ \overrightarrow{OA} = 2\mathbf{i} + 6\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}, \quad \overrightarrow{OB} = 5\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{k} \quad \text{and} \quad \overrightarrow{OC} = 5\mathbf{i} - 14\mathbf{j} + 8\mathbf{k}. $$
The line $l$ passes through the points $A$ and $B$.

**(a)** Find the length of the perpendicular from $C$ to $l$. **[5]**

The line $m$ has vector equation $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k} + \mu (a\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k})$, where $a$ is an integer.
The acute angle between the directions of lines $l$ and $m$ is equal to $\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{4}{21}\right)$.

**(b)** Find the value of $a$. **[5]**

### Question 10
**(a)** It is given that $5\cot 2x - 3\cot^2 x = 0$. Using the substitution $u = \tan x$, where $u \neq 0$, form an equation in $u$ and hence show that $u = \sqrt[3]{u - 1.2}$. **[4]**

**(b)** Given that there is exactly one real value of $u$ satisfying the equation $u = \sqrt[3]{u - 1.2}$, verify by calculation that this value lies between $-2$ and $-1$. **[2]**

**(c)** Use the iterative formula $u_{n+1} = \sqrt[3]{u_n - 1.2}$, together with initial value $u_1 = -1.5$, to find the value of $u$ correct to $3$ decimal places. Give the result of each iteration to $5$ decimal places. **[3]**

**(d)** Using the value of $u$ found in part **(c)**, solve the equation $5\cot 2x - 3\cot^2 x = 0$ for $-\pi < x < \pi$.
Give your answers correct to three significant figures. **[2]**

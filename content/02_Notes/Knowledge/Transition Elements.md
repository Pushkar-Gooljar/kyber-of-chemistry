# Chemistry of transition elements
>[!info] Define a transition element 
>A d-block element that forms one or more stable ions with incomplete 3d orbitals.

>[!info] Define the term degenerate 
>Orbitals are at the same energy.

>[!info] State the full electronic configuration of $\ce{Cu^2+}$
>$\mathrm{1s^22s^22p^63s^23p^63d^9}$

>[!info] State the colour of the aqueous solution of $\ce{[Cu(NH_{3})4(H_{2}O)2]^2+}$
>Deep blue

>[!info] State the colour of the aqueous solution of $\ce{[CuCl_{4}]^2-}$
>Yellow


>[!info] Explain why aqueous complex ions of transition elements are usually coloured.
>- The presence of ligands cause the d-subshell to split into two sets of non-degenerate d-orbitals.
>- An electron is promoted from a lower-energy d-orbital to a higher-energy d-orbital
>- By absorbing a photon having a specific frequency of visible light
>- This frequency is missing from the transmitted light therefore the complementary colour is seen.

>[!info] Define the stability constant of a complex
>Equilibrium constant for the formation of the complex ion in a solvent from its constituent ions or molecules.

>[!info] State the relationship between stability of a complex ion and its stability constant
>The higher the stability constant the more stable the complex ion.

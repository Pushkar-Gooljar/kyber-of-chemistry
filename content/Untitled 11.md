To systematically build out your Obsidian Canvas starting from your single **Benzene** node, you can structure your expansion in tiers. Below is a step-by-step guide to adding the direct derivatives of benzene (Tier 1) and their subsequent reactions (Tier 2), incorporating the reagents, conditions, mechanisms, and equations specified in the 9701 syllabus and past papers.

---

### **Tier 1: Direct Extensions from Benzene**

These are the immediate synthetic routes from the **Benzene** node. For each route, create a **Target Node** (representing the product) and an **Explainer Node** (placed on the arrow connecting Benzene to the product).

#### **1. Route to Methylbenzene (Friedel-Crafts Alkylation)**
*   **Target Node:** `Methylbenzene`
*   **Explainer Node:**
    *   **Process Name:** Friedel–Crafts Alkylation
    *   **Reagents:** Halogenoalkane (e.g., Chloromethane, $\text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$)
    *   **Conditions:** Anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$ catalyst, heat
    *   **Mechanism Type:** Electrophilic substitution
    *   **Equation:** $$\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{Cl} \xrightarrow{\text{AlCl}_3} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3 + \text{HCl}$$

#### **2. Route to Phenylethanone (Friedel-Crafts Acylation)**
*   **Target Node:** `Phenylethanone`
*   **Explainer Node:**
    *   **Process Name:** Friedel–Crafts Acylation
    *   **Reagents:** Acyl chloride (e.g., Ethanoyl chloride, $\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$)
    *   **Conditions:** Anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$ catalyst, heat
    *   **Mechanism Type:** Electrophilic substitution
    *   **Equation:** $$\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{COCl} \xrightarrow{\text{AlCl}_3} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COCH}_3 + \text{HCl}$$

#### **3. Route to Nitrobenzene (Nitration)**
*   **Target Node:** `Nitrobenzene`
*   **Explainer Node:**
    *   **Process Name:** Nitration
    *   **Reagents:** Concentrated $\text{HNO}_3$ and concentrated $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$
    *   **Conditions:** Temperature between $25^\circ\text{C}$ and $55^\circ\text{C}$ (to prevent further substitution)
    *   **Mechanism Type:** Electrophilic substitution
    *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{HNO}_3 \xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{NO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$

#### **4. Route to Chlorobenzene / Bromobenzene (Halogenation)**
*   **Target Node:** `Chlorobenzene` (or `Bromobenzene`)
*   **Explainer Node:**
    *   **Process Name:** Halogenation
    *   **Reagents:** Chlorine gas ($\text{Cl}_2$) or Bromine liquid ($\text{Br}_2$)
    *   **Conditions:** Anhydrous halogen carrier catalyst (e.g., $\text{AlCl}_3$, $\text{FeCl}_3$, $\text{AlBr}_3$, or $\text{FeBr}_3$), room temperature, absence of UV light
    *   **Mechanism Type:** Electrophilic substitution
    *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{Cl}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{AlCl}_3} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{Cl} + \text{HCl}$

#### **5. Route to Cyclohexane (Reduction / Hydrogenation)**
*   **Target Node:** `Cyclohexane`
*   **Explainer Node:**
    *   **Process Name:** Catalytic Hydrogenation
    *   **Reagents:** Hydrogen gas ($\text{H}_2$)
    *   **Conditions:** Nickel ($\text{Ni}$) or Platinum ($\text{Pt}$) catalyst, heat
    *   **Mechanism Type:** Addition
    *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + 3\text{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{Ni, heat}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}$

### **Tier 2: Secondary Extensions (Expanding Outwards)**

Now you can build pathways stemming from your Tier 1 nodes to capture multi-step synthetic routes covered by the syllabus.

#### **From the "Methylbenzene" Node:**

*   **Pathway A: To Benzoic Acid (Oxidation of Side-Chain)**
    *   **Target Node:** `Benzoic Acid`
    *   **Explainer Node:**
        *   **Process Name:** Side-Chain Oxidation
        *   **Reagents:** Hot alkaline potassium manganate(VII) ($\text{KMnO}_4$), followed by dilute acid (e.g., dilute $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$)
        *   **Conditions:** Reflux under heat
        *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3 + 3[\text{O}] \rightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COOH} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$

*   **Pathway B: To (Chloromethyl)benzene (Side-Chain Halogenation)**
    *   **Target Node:** `(Chloromethyl)benzene`
    *   **Explainer Node:**
        *   **Process Name:** Free Radical Substitution
        *   **Reagents:** Chlorine gas ($\text{Cl}_2$)
        *   **Conditions:** UV light or heat
        *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3 + \text{Cl}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{UV light}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_2\text{Cl} + \text{HCl}$

#### **From the "Nitrobenzene" Node:**

*   **Pathway C: To Phenylamine (Reduction)**
    *   **Target Node:** `Phenylamine`
    *   **Explainer Node:**
        *   **Process Name:** Reduction
        *   **Reagents:** Tin ($\text{Sn}$) and concentrated hydrochloric acid ($\text{HCl}$), followed by addition of aqueous sodium hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$)
        *   **Conditions:** Heat under reflux
        *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{NO}_2 + 6[\text{H}] \rightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{NH}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$

#### **From the "Phenylamine" Node:**

*   **Pathway D: To Benzenediazonium Chloride (Diazotisation)**
    *   **Target Node:** `Benzenediazonium Chloride`
    *   **Explainer Node:**
        *   **Process Name:** Diazotisation
        *   **Reagents:** Nitrous acid ($\text{HNO}_2$), generated in-situ from sodium nitrite ($\text{NaNO}_2$) and dilute acid ($\text{HCl}$)
        *   **Conditions:** Keep temperature strictly below $10^\circ\text{C}$ (ideally $0^\circ\text{C} - 5^\circ\text{C}$)
        *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{NH}_2 + \text{HNO}_2 + \text{HCl} \xrightarrow{<10^\circ\text{C}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{N}_2^+\text{Cl}^- + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$

#### **From the "Benzenediazonium Chloride" Node:**

*   **Pathway E: To Phenol (Hydrolysis / Diazonium Decomposition)**
    *   **Target Node:** `Phenol`
    *   **Explainer Node:**
        *   **Process Name:** Thermal decomposition / Hydrolysis
        *   **Reagents:** Water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$)
        *   **Conditions:** Warm temperature above $10^\circ\text{C}$
        *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{N}_2^+\text{Cl}^- + \text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{>10^\circ\text{C}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{OH} + \text{N}_2 + \text{HCl}$

*   **Pathway F: To an Azo Dye (Coupling)**
    *   **Target Node:** `Azo Compound` (e.g., 4-phenylazophenol)
    *   **Explainer Node:**
        *   **Process Name:** Coupling Reaction
        *   **Reagents:** Phenol dissolved in aqueous sodium hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$) to form sodium phenoxide
        *   **Conditions:** Cold temperature below $10^\circ\text{C}$
        *   **Equation:** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{N}_2^+\text{Cl}^- + \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{O}^-\text{Na}^+ \rightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{N=NC}_6\text{H}_4\text{OH} + \text{NaCl}$
